# Review of port/src/engine for the final refactor PRD

Read-only review, 2026-10-16. Every `.c` and `.h` of `port/src/engine/` was read in full (12,675 lines, 19 modules), with grep evidence from `ui/`, `platform/`, `tests/` and `docs/`. No build, no tests. Line numbers are from the tree at commit `0771406`.

Sizes: S under an hour, M a few hours, L a day or more. Game behavior does not change in any proposal; where a proposal touches a rule path (F4, F29) the differential and golden tests are the safety net.

## What is good and should stay

- The four-layer order and its mechanical check; no engine file includes `ui/`. The hook pattern (`g_endgame_sensor_hook`) is used once and documented.
- Named constants with game-terms comments: `champion.c:19-93`, `group.c:16-74`, `magic.c:17-47`, `inventory.c:14-54`. A reader learns the rule from the constant block.
- The split of the 500-line original creature AI into `GroupTurn` phases (`group.c:1346-2054`) and `GroupMove` (`group.c:2089-2241`); `SquareChange` + `scan_square` in `sensor.c:315-345`; `BitReader`/`TextOut` in `dungeon.c:70-107, 496-555`.
- Event-word accessors (`timeline_process.h:74-119`, `projectile.h:102-123`): `b`/`c` are no longer unpacked at call sites.
- Kept-quirk and bug-fix comments are consistent and short (`group.c:383, 1266, 1950`, `champion.c:335`, `projectile.c:750`).
- The timeline heap (`game.c:183-378`) is small, documented, and correct; `goto` appears once, as the standard allows (`savefile.c:111-134`).
- Per-module `*_reset` functions and `game_reset_session` (`game.c:91`).
- `savefile.c` (container, crypto) vs `savegame.c` (contents) is the right cut; only the names need work (F7).
- `message.c`, `random.c`, `recruit.c`, `explored.c` are short and readable.
- `tests/` bind 335 engine symbols through ctypes: the differential tests are the reason this refactor is possible at all. Every rename below must update them in the same commit.

## 1. Module boundaries and cohesion

1. **game.h is a grab bag.** Seven prefixes on one header (`game.h:3`), four structs that belong to other modules (`Champion` 70, `Party` 136, `TimelineEvent` 146, `ActiveGroup` 154), 29 globals, the timeline heap (`game.c:183-378`), arithmetic helpers (`game.c:151-172`), champion statistic rules (`game.c:392-435`), active-group allocation (`game.c:381`). Why: a reader cannot tell what "game" owns; every ui file includes it (13 of 16) and gets `tables.h` and `bytes.h` with it. Proposal: `timeline.h/.c` (TimelineEvent, heap, `g_timeline*`), `party.h` (Party, position word, `g_party_*` from dungeon.h), Champion and the statistic helpers into `champion.h/.c`, `min16/max16/bounded_value/scale_value` as `static inline` in a small `arith.h`; `game.h` keeps session state (time, mode, cheats, redraw). **L**

2. **Record layout is split over four headers although rule 2 names one owner.** `map.h` indexes records 48 times, `group.h` 14 (`group.h:93-141`), `projectile.h` 10 (`projectile.h:56-92`), `dungeon.c` 2 (`dungeon.c:286, 564`). The layout constants are split the same way: `GROUP_DIRECTION_BYTE`/`GROUP_BEHAVIOR_BYTE` in `map.h:424` but `GROUP_RECORD_*_MASK` in `group.h:86-92`; `CREATURE_LEVITATES`/`CREATURE_NON_MATERIAL` in `map.h:300` and the other creature attribute bits in `group.h:16-27` (the comment at `group.h:15` admits it); sensor types 7-10, 14, 15 in `projectile.h:40-47`, the rest in `map.h:121-135`; `EXPLOSION_RECORD_TYPE_MASK` (`projectile.h:78`) repeats the bare `& 0x7f` of `map.h:451`. `map.h` itself has two passes over the same records: group/projectile/explosion at 299-314 and again under "fields the view reads" at 417-456. The `test_standard` RECORD regex matches `dungeon_get_thing_data(...)[` and `g_current_map[` only, so `REC(x)[...]` (72 uses outside map.c) and `g_current_map_data[x][y]` (17 uses in group.c, projectile.c, timeline_process.c, sensor.c, move.c) pass. Why: a layout change needs edits in four files; the check does not protect what the rule says. Proposal: one `record.h/.c` (or `things.h`) with all getters and setters per thing type in thing-type order, `REC` private to it; `map.h` keeps squares, directions and the current map; the check matches `REC(` and `g_current_map_data[` outside the owner. **L**

3. **Helpers duplicated across modules.** `is_party_on` four times (`group.c:101`, `projectile.c:50`, `timeline_process.c:33`, `move.c:102`); `print_text` twice (`timeline_process.c:54`, `sensor.c:307`); "print System Error and exit" three times (`game.c:56`, `map.c:157`, `group.c:307`); "one square ahead" three times (`group.c:111 step`, `action.c:187 step_forward`, `champion.c:198 get_square_in_front`); "champion faces the party direction" three times (`action.c:195`, `magic.c:193`, `champion.c:1125`); the side-of-cell formula `… & 2) >> 1` four times (`group.c:1021, 2141`, `move.c:179`, `projectile.c:859`); "first thing of a type on a square" loops seven times (`map.c:139, 244`, `recruit.c:101`, `champion.c:457`, `sensor.c:81`, `projectile.c:569, 751`). Proposal: `map_is_party_on`, `map_step`, `map_get_square_first_thing_of_type`, `champion_face_party_direction`, `cell_beside_direction` in their owning headers; one `game_fatal(code)`. **M**

4. **Two copies of the teleporter/pit/stairs chain.** `follow_destination_squares` (`group.c:2099-2147`) and `follow_square_chain` (`move.c:148-186`) walk the same chain with the same stairs cell formula; `group_get_move_result` (`group.c:2194`) and `move_get_move_result` (`move.c:217`) are parallel 50-line functions. `map_at_level` (`dungeon.c:290`) repeats `map_get_location_after_level_change` (`map.c:86`); `dungeon_get_square_first_thing` (`dungeon.c:467`) repeats `map_get_square_first_thing_index` (`map.c:121`). Why: a fix in one copy misses the other; the original has one `move_get_move_result`, the split was the port's choice. Proposal: one chain walker with a `MoverKind` (party, group, object, projectile) and one `move_get_move_result` that calls group-specific hooks; `dungeon_validate` uses the map.c functions on a temporarily selected map. **L** (rule path; the group differential tests cover it)

5. **Misplaced state and code.** `g_dungeon_view_palette_index` is declared in `group.h:213`, defined in `group.c:81`, written only by `inventory.c:580-589`; the light rules `inventory_get_party_light`, `inventory_set_dungeon_view_palette`, `inventory_decrease_torches_light_power` (`inventory.c:546-606`) are party light, not inventory. `g_party_map_x/y/direction/map_index` and `g_event_max_count` live in the dungeon loader (`dungeon.h:22-23`, `dungeon.c:17-18`). `action_reset` clears `g_redraw` (`action.c:174`; in todo.md). Static functions with another module's prefix: `champion_face_party_direction` in `action.c:195`, `move_get_teleporter_rotated_group_result` in `group.c:2058`, `map_at_level`/`map_raw_data_offset` in `dungeon.c:290, 142`, `map_square_count`/`map_first_square` in `explored.c:14-19`. Proposal: `light.c` (or `party.c`) owns the palette index and the light rules; party position to `party.h`; statics without module prefix (F15). **M**

6. **sound.c is half rules, half Paula emulator.** Rules: `sound_request_play`, the pending queue, `sound_get_volume` (`sound.c:63-205`). Renderer: `Voice`, `sound_mix`, `sound_music_*`, `sound_play_data`, `sound_demo_play` (`sound.c:20-39, 207-286`), called from `platform/platform.c:248`, `ui/anim.c:84,173,225,334`, `ui/logo.c:28,45`. Why: an engine module owns audio output; the shared library used by the tests carries a mixer. Proposal: engine keeps request, queue, volume and calls a `sound_play_hook(data, length, period, volumes)`; the mixer and music move to `platform/mixer.c` (ui owns the music sequencing it already drives). **M**

7. **savefile vs savegame: right cut, wrong names and ownership.** `savefile.h` uses prefix `save_` (`savefile.h:3`), not `savefile_`; `SaveFile.data` is owned after `save_decode` but borrowed when `encode_game` builds the struct on the stack (`savegame.c:253-260`), with no field saying which; `savegame.c` does its own file IO (`fopen/rename/remove`, `savegame.c:322-332, 583`) while `data/file.h` has only `file_read` and `file_exists`. Proposal: prefix `savefile_`; `file_write` and `file_replace` in `data/file.c`; a `bool owns_data` or a separate `SaveFileView` for the borrowed case. **S-M**

8. **Timeline is split between game.c and timeline_process.c, with 13 exported handlers nobody else calls.** Heap operations `timeline_*` are declared in `game.h:210-220`; the dispatcher `event_process_expired` (`timeline_process.c:506`) and its 13 `event_process_*` handlers are exported (`timeline_process.h:126-139`), but only the dispatcher calls them (grep: zero callers outside, one test uses `event_process_wall`). Proposal: `timeline.c/.h` (heap) and `event.c/.h` (dispatcher plus static handlers); drop the `_process` suffix from the file name. **M**

9. **message.c measures pixels inside the engine.** `MESSAGE_ROW_WIDTH 320`, `MESSAGE_WRAP_INDENT 12`, `TEXT_CHAR_WIDTH` from `graphics.h` (`message.h:5-14`), `int` cursor arithmetic (`message.c:78-109`), and every print passes `g_game_time` as a parameter (`champion.c:192-195`, `magic.c:109-112`, `sensor.c:58`, `action.c:224`; ui does the same, `ui/advanced.c:116-117`). The original wraps text in the rules, so the model may stay; the parameter and the `int` types are the problem. Proposal: drop the `game_time` parameter (read the global, as every other rule does), `int16_t` for columns, and a two-line header comment that says the row model is the rule-level one. **S**

10. **explored.c (port-only) uses another error convention.** `explored_save`/`explored_load` return `int` 0/-1 (`explored.h:20-21`) where the layer returns `bool`; its `SAVE_VERSION`, `SAVE_HEADER_SIZE` (`explored.c:6`) read like `savefile.h` constants (`SAVE_HEADER_BYTES`). Proposal: `bool` and an `EXPLORED_` prefix. **S**

## 2. Naming

11. **Four naming schemes for record accessors.** `rec_<type>_<field>` (sensor, text, group, projectile, explosion, object, dungeon), `<type>_<field>` (`door_`, `teleporter_`, `weapon_`, `armour_`, `junk_`, `potion_`, `container_`, `scroll_`), `<module>_<field>` (`group_possessions`, `group_health`, `projectile_attack`, `explosion_set_type`), and `thing_weapon_type` (`map.h:412`) next to `weapon_type` (`map.c:285`) - two names for the same field (ui/view.c uses the first, engine the second). `group_get_cells` (`group.c:125`, active-aware), `rec_group_cells_byte` (`map.h:420`) and `group_set_cells_byte` (`group.h:105`) are three spellings of one concept. Rule 7 says accessors are nouns, but `map_get_square`, `group_get_cells` are verbs. Proposal: one scheme `<type>_<field>` / `<type>_set_<field>` for record fields, drop `rec_`, delete `thing_weapon_type`; the active-aware pair becomes `group_cells_on_map`/`group_set_cells_on_map`; write the scheme into the standard. **M** (mechanical rename; `docs/` and the ctypes tests follow)

12. **Predicates that return int16_t without `is_`.** `door_type`, `door_breaks_by_magic`, `door_breaks_by_force`, `door_button_present` (`map.h:259-274`); `rec_sensor_revert` (`map.h:171`) is used as a bool at `sensor.c:297` and `timeline_process.c:230`; `teleporter_is_rotation_absolute` is stored in `int16_t absolute` (`group.c:2061`). The check only looks at names with `is_/has_/can_`. Proposal: `door_is_breakable_by_magic`, `door_has_button`, `sensor_is_reverted`, returning `bool`. **S**

13. **Byte-offset enums with one-letter prefixes.** `G_GAME_TIME …` (`savefile.h:16-35`) reads as a global; `H_*` (`savefile.c:10-26`), `P_*`, `E_*`, `A_*` (`savegame.c:26-44`), `REC_*` (`champion.c:106-124`), which collides visually with the `REC()` macro that `champion.c` includes through `map.h`. Proposal: `SAVE_GLOBAL_*`, `SAVE_HEADER_*`, `SAVE_PARTY_*`, `SAVE_EVENT_*`, `SAVE_GROUP_*`, `CHAMPION_RECORD_*`. **S**

14. **Names that still carry the decompiler.** `game_update_map_coordinates_after_relative_movement` (`game.h:207`, wrapped three times, F3); `x_combo`/`y_combo` (`projectile.h:133, 137`); `BEHAVIOR_USELESS_2/_3` (`group.h:59-60`); `ACTION_STAB_1/_2`; `SLOT_QUIVER_LINE2_1` and `SLOT_POUCH_2` listed before `SLOT_POUCH_1` (`game.h:55-61`); `event->b`/`event->c` read raw in 20+ places (`group.c:279, 1439, 2249`, `projectile.c:80, 505`, `timeline_process.c:473-500`, `magic.c:51`, `sound.c:185`); `g_group_secondary_direction` as a second return value (`group.c:1115-1144`); `OUTCOME_*` as a 0/1/2 int; the "ordinal" vocabulary (F24). Proposal: `map_step(direction, forward, right, &x, &y)`; a `Square {x, y}` pair instead of combos (F21); an accessor for every `b`/`c` use so the raw fields appear only in `timeline.c` and `savegame.c`. **M**

15. **Static functions carry module prefixes at random.** `group.c`: 20 of 62 statics have `group_`, `move_` or `random_` prefixes (`group_is_movement_possible` at 868 is static, `random_move` at 1569 is static too); `action.c` 2 of 14, `dungeon.c` 3 of 27, `sensor.c` 1, `sound.c` 1, the other files 0. Rule 7 says nothing about statics. Proposal: rule "a static function has no module prefix"; rename about 27 functions; the prefix check can verify it. **S**

16. **`THING_END` is a second name for `THING_ENDOFLIST`** (`map.h:55`), used 27 times (`projectile.c` 15, `inventory.c` 5, `ui/view.c` 5, `champion.c` 1, `map.c` 1). Rule 1 asks for one name. Proposal: keep `THING_ENDOFLIST`, delete the alias. **S**

17. **Two local enums for one table field, and an unnamed skill.** Weapon classes: `CLASS_SWING_WEAPON … CLASS_AMMUNITION_END` (`champion.c:96`) and `WEAPON_CLASS_*` (`action.c:24-32`) describe `WeaponInfo.weapon_class`. `SENSOR_EXPERIENCE_SKILL 8 /* steal */` (`sensor.c:14`): the skill enum in `data/tables.h` has gaps (`SKILL_SWING = 4`, `SKILL_PARRY = 7`, nothing at 8). Proposal: `WEAPON_CLASS_*` next to `WeaponInfo` in `data/tables.h`; complete the `SKILL_*` enum (`SKILL_STEAL = 8`). **S**

## 3. Types and API shape

18. **One game value, several types.** `champion_index` is `int16_t` in 18 declarations and `uint16_t` in 24. `int16_t`: `champion_set_portrait`, `champion_set_leader`, `champion_move_to_cell`, `champion_get_skill_level`, `champion_get_strength`, `champion_get_wound_defense`, `champion_add_pending_damage_and_wounds`, `champion_poison`, `champion_unpoison`, `champion_decrement_stamina` (`champion.h:67-111`), `group_get_melee_action_damage` (`group.h:267`), `magic_set_caster` (`magic.h:46`), `projectile_shoot_from_champion` (`projectile.h:152`). `uint16_t`: `champion_resurrect`, `champion_add_skill_experience`, `champion_kill`, `champion_drop_all_objects`, `champion_add_redraw_flags`, all nine `action_*`, both `inventory_*`, five `magic_*`, `group_get_champion_damage`, `event_enable_champion_action`, `event_hide_damage_received`. The globals `g_leader_index`, `g_magic_caster_champion_index` are `int16_t` (-1 none), `g_party_champion_count` is `uint16_t`, so loops compare signs (`champion.c:264 i != g_leader_index`). Same split for `slot` (3 `int16_t` / 3 `uint16_t`), `cell` (13 / 3), `direction` (8 `int16_t` / 2 `uint16_t` / 1 `uint8_t`), `ticks` (1 / 4 / 1 `uint8_t`), `mode` (2 / 1). Rule 3 ("one value has one type on its whole path") is not checked. Proposal: write the table into the standard (`champion_index`, `cell`, `direction`, `map`, `slot`: `int16_t`, -1 none; `ticks`, `thing`: `uint16_t`), fix the headers, and add a check that a parameter name has one type across engine headers. **M**

19. **Truth values typed `int16_t` or `int`** (rule 3 says `bool`; the check covers only `is_/has_/can_` names). `group.c`: `absolute` 2061, `points_to_itself` 2110, `all_killed`/`some_killed` 648-649, `spell` 1014, `both_visible` 1129, `retried` 2260, `GroupTurn.moved/was_attacking/archenemy` 1358-1362; `projectile.c`: `single` 766, `solid` 458, `weakened_to_nothing` 334, `hit` 719; `champion.c:522 int temporary`; `move.c:67-68 keep_larger/found`; `action.c:498 side`; `recruit.h:14 uint8_t male`; parameter `magic_attack` of `event_is_door_destroyed_by_attack` (`timeline_process.h:140`; callers pass 0/1); `ticks` of `group_process_reaction_event` receives `false` (`group.c:675`, `projectile.c:525`). Proposal: fix the ~20 sites; extend the check to locals assigned from a comparison or from `true`/`false`. **S**

20. **Overloaded return codes.** `group_get_move_result` returns 0, 1, or "active index + 2" (`group.c:2191-2193`, decoded at 2183); `move_get_move_result` "returns 0" but returns 1 at `move.c:230`; `click_test` returns 0, 1 or `CLICK_IGNORED` under the name `skip` (`sensor.c:184-239`); `hit_creature` 0/1/2 (`projectile.c:229-266`); `savegame_save` returns `int16_t` 1 while `savegame_save_as` returns `bool` (`savegame.h:17-22`); `savegame_quick_save` (todo.md). Proposal: `enum MoveResult`, `enum ClickResult`, `enum HitResult`; `bool` for the saves. **S-M**

21. **Overloaded parameters and long lists.** `group_process_reaction_event(x, y, event_type, ticks)` takes an `EVENT_*` 29-41 or a `REACTION_*` -3..-1 (`group.h:70`, `group.c:1999`); `sound_request_play(…, mode)`: 0 and 1 are modes, 2 and up mean "ticks + 1" (`sound.c:176-188`); `move_get_move_result` encodes "add only", "remove only" and "no rotation" in negative coordinates (`move.c:215`, `NO_TELEPORTER_ROTATION -2` passed from `projectile.c:172`); `champion_get_skill_level(index, skill | flags)` and `champion_get_wound_defense(index, slot | WOUND_DEFENSE_USE_SHARP)` (`champion.h:95, 101`). Lists over five: `projectile_create` 8, `group_get_melee_action_damage` 8, `create_impact_explosion` 7, `hit_creature` 7, `group_get_generated` 6, `sensor_add_event` 6, `is_teleported` 6, `follow_square_chain` 6, `remove_dead_creature` 6, `arrive_on_square` 6; about 40 signatures carry `x, y` or `src_x, src_y, dst_x, dst_y`. Proposal: a `Square {int16_t x, y;}` (and `Position {map, x, y}`) value type in `map.h`; separate `bool` or enum parameters; the standard gets "at most five parameters, else a struct". **M-L**

22. **Index and pointer APIs mixed in champion.h.** `champion_get_maximum_load`, `champion_get_movement_ticks`, `champion_get_dexterity` take `const Champion *`; `champion_get_strength`, `champion_get_wound_defense`, `champion_get_skill_level` take an index (`champion.h:95-101`). Proposal: one convention (index, because the rules also need the index for events and messages). **S**

23. **Output parameters where a small struct is clearer.** `game_get_magic_map_position(spells, *map, *x, *y)` (`game.h:128`); `map_get_location_after_level_change` returns the map and rewrites `*x, *y` (`map.h:395`); `get_creature_spell_explosion(type, *spell_sound)` (`group.c:979`); `get_object_modifier(…, *stat)` (`inventory.c:203`); `get_flask_explosion(thing, *attack)` (`projectile.c:304`); `cast_magic_map(…, *disabled_ticks)` (`magic.c:303`); `is_material_group_on(x, y, *attributes)` (`timeline_process.c:39`); `click_test(uint16_t *t, …)` rewrites the caller's loop cursor (`sensor.c:184, 207`). Proposal: return `Position`, `{explosion, attack}`, `{stat, bonus}`; `click_test` returns the next thing. **S-M**

24. **Ordinals (index + 1, 0 = none) are still the representation.** `g_inventory_champion_ordinal`, `g_acting_champion_ordinal`, `g_candidate_champion_ordinal`, `group_get_creature_ordinal_in_cell`, `group_get_smelled_party_direction_ordinal`, `group_get_first_possible_movement_direction_ordinal`, `group_get_party_scent_ordinal`: 39 `ordinal - 1` conversions (engine 17, ui 22) and six `champion_index + 1 == g_…_ordinal` tests. Proposal: indices with -1 for none (`g_inventory_champion_index`); ordinals only where a record stores them. **M** (ui and tests follow)

25. **`tables_get_*` returns pointers to scalars**, dereferenced at every call: `*tables_get_slot_mask(slot)` (`champion.c:297, 301, 319`, `inventory.c:371`), `*tables_get_light_power_to_light_amount` (6 sites), `*tables_get_wound_defense_factor`, `*tables_get_junk_weight`, `*tables_get_food_value`, `*tables_get_symbol_base_mana_cost`, `*tables_get_fixed_possession`. A `data/` API, but the noise lands in the engine. Proposal: return values. **S** (data owner)

## 4. Global mutable state

26. **Count.** 101 `g_` names in engine headers: `game.h` 29, `dungeon.h` 17, `group.h` 9, `move.h` 8, `action.h` 6, `champion.h` 6, `map.h` 6, `sensor.h` 4, `inventory.h` 4, `projectile.h` 4, `savegame.h` 3, `sound.h` 2, `timeline_process.h` 2, `message.h` 1, `random.h` 1. 36 of them have no reader in `ui/` or `platform/`.

35. **Split badly around the 60-line limit.** 34 functions are 45 lines or longer (`projectile_has_impact_occurred` 59, `explosion_process_step_event` 59, `projectile_process_move_event` 58, `drink_potion` 58, `process_creature_behavior` 58, `group_is_movement_possible` 58, `click_test` 56, …). ## 9. Other items a senior C reviewer flags

46. **Right shifts of negative values are implementation-defined** (not UB): `(int16_t)experience >> 1` (`action.c:563, 597, 612`), `product >> shift` (`game.c:171`), `(int16_t)-stamina >> 1` (`champion.c:952`), `value >> 1` (`game.c:397`), `delta >> 2` (`game.c:417`). clang and GCC on the two targets shift arithmetically, which the port needs. Proposal: one sentence in the standard and a `static_assert((-1 >> 1) == -1, …)` in `bytes.h`. **S**

47. **Const-correctness stops at the record edge.** `uint8_t *dungeon_get_thing_data(uint16_t)` (`dungeon.h:36`) is the only record pointer, so every read accessor gets a writable pointer and `const` cannot propagate; `timeline_add_event(TimelineEvent *)` mutates its argument through the merge (`game.c:299-337`), which no caller reads back; `sound_request_play` and friends take non-const where const would do. Proposal: `const uint8_t *record_read(thing)` plus `uint8_t *record_write(thing)`; `const TimelineEvent *` where no merge is possible. **S**

48. **Header hygiene.** Include guards are bare `MAP_H`, `GAME_H`, `SOUND_H`, `RANDOM_H`, `MESSAGE_H` (collision-prone; `DM_ENGINE_MAP_H`); include order is system-first in `savegame.c:1-3`, `savefile.c:6-7`, `magic.c:1`, `message.c:1-2`, `sound.c:1-2` and project-first elsewhere (`SortIncludes: Never` leaves it to hand); `game.h` pulls `tables.h` and `bytes.h` into 13 ui files; `inventory.h` includes `map.h` for `THING_NONE`, `message.h` includes `graphics.h` for one constant; `map.h` carries 72 `static inline` bodies and `group.h` 27, compiled into every includer; `champion.h:43` holds `PANEL_*` click ids (ui vocabulary). Proposal: `DM_` guard prefix rule, include-order rule (own header, project, system), forward declarations instead of `tables.h` in `game.h`. **S**

49. **ui reaches engine internals through exposed arrays.** `ui/view.c:674-675` indexes `g_active_groups[index]`; `view.c:894, 2338` read `g_timeline_events[rec_projectile_event_index(thing)]`. Proposal: `group_get_active(index)` and `projectile_get_direction(thing)`; then `g_active_groups` and `g_timeline_events` leave the public headers. **S**

50. **Formatter artifacts show where the shape fights the tool.** `savegame.c:48-49` (a trailing comment longer than the column limit splits a declaration), `group.h:123-137`, `projectile.h:85-91` (`REC()[...]` lvalues). Both disappear with F40 and shorter comments. **S**
